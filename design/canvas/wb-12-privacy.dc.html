Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

For each child: a nickname chosen from a list, an age band, a country and their progress in the game. For the parent: an email address, used to sign in and to send one-time codes.

Why we collect it

So the game can show the right level, coins and examples, and so a parent can follow progress from their own phone.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no third-party tracking on children's profiles.
  • No precise location, contacts or photos.
  • No chat between users.
  • No real money, and no way to buy anything.

How long we keep it

An account with no sign-in or play for 12 months is deleted automatically. We email you 30 days and 7 days before.

Your choices

You can export or delete your family's data at any time from the Parent Dashboard.
