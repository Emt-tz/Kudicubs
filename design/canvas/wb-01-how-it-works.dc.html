How KudiCubs works

Short missions, one choice at a time, and a friendly lion cub to guide the way.

  1. 1

    Play a mission

    Each mission is a little story, such as a lost-coins market walk. Children touch, sort and decide.

  2. 2

    See what happens

    Every choice has a visible result. Save a coin and the jar grows. Price a snack too high and fewer friends buy it.

  3. 3

    Grow and earn

    Pretend coins, XP and badges mark progress. Nothing earned is ever taken away.

Rewards that mean something

Coins, XP, badges and levels celebrate effort. They are fictional and cannot be bought, sold or turned into real money.

Five skills that grow together

Know

What money is and what it is for.

Understand

Why prices, costs and choices matter.

Decide

Weigh choices and live with the result.

Invest

Put effort and coins to work over time.

Share

Give, help and enjoy doing it.
