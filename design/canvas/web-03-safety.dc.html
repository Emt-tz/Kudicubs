How we keep children safe

Plain answers to what parents ask first. The grown-up area is for you, and the game is for them.

A grown-up gate and a PIN

Settings, account changes and anything outside the game sit behind a gate a young child cannot pass by chance, then your own PIN.

No real money

Coins are pretend. They cannot be bought, sold or turned into real money, and children cannot make purchases.

No chatting with strangers

There is no open chat and nothing children type is shown to other users. Names come from a short list.

Only what the game needs

A nickname, an age band, a country and progress. No precise location, contacts or photos.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising and no third-party tracking on children's profiles.

Mistakes are safe

Under 7s never see a failure screen. Older children can always try again and never lose what they have earned.
