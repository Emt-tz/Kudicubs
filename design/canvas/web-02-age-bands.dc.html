One game that grows with your child

Every age band has the same structure and a different level of challenge. A parent sets the band and can change it at any time.

Ages 4 to 6

Money & Choices

Pictures and sounds, no reading needed. Children find, sort and count coins, then choose to save or spend.

Example: The Lost Coins.

Ages 7 to 9

Build & Grow

Short sentences and two things to weigh at once, such as planning a party on a budget. A try-again button is always there.

Example: Birthday Party budget.

Ages 10 to 12

Founder Mode

More choices at once: a loan, an investor or savings. Children see how each choice changes the outcome.

Example: Clothing Brand funding.

Ages 13 to 15

Side Hustle

Cost, price and profit with a small budget. Real-feeling trade-offs, always with pretend coins.

Example: Side Hustle first sale.

Money that looks like home

Coins and examples follow where your family lives. Pick a country in the parent area and the game changes with it.
