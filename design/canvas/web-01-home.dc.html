Play. Earn. Roar!

KudiCubs is a play-and-learn app that teaches children aged 4 to 15 how money works. Short missions, a friendly lion cub called Kudi, and pretend coins only.

Get the app

Why parents like it

Learning by doing

Children sort coins, run a little stall and make choices. They learn what money is for, how to save it and how to share it, by playing.

Safe by design

No real money, no chatting with strangers, no ads. Settings sit behind a parent gate.

How we keep children safe

Grows with your child

Four age bands, from picture-only play at 4 to a first side hustle at 13. Money examples match where you live.

Made for ages 4 to 15

4 to 6

Money & Choices

Pictures and sounds. No reading needed.

7 to 9

Build & Grow

Short sentences, small businesses and saving goals.

10 to 12

Founder Mode

Real ideas such as loans and profit, made simple.

13 to 15

Side Hustle

Trade-offs, goals and smart spending.
