Review guide

How to open each platform

  • App: use a phone in portrait. On a laptop, narrow the window to phone width. Hold the KudiCubs logo for 2 seconds to open the reviewer menu (country, voice, jump to a screen, load sample data, restart).
  • Website: works on a phone, a tablet and a laptop.
  • Dashboards: the demo email is parent@example.com and the demo code is 123456. A reviewer button on each view lets you force the wrong-code, expired, empty and error states.

Things to know

  • The voice you hear in the app is a scratch voice for demos only. Human recordings must replace it before any public release.
  • One fictional family appears everywhere, so numbers match between the app and the dashboards.
  • The legal pages hold placeholder text pending review.
  • This set is kept out of search engines.
