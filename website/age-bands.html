What each age band does

The same game, made for each age. Picture-only at 4, more reading and thinking as children grow.

Ages 4 to 6

Money & Choices

Pictures, sounds and a spoken guide, so no reading is needed. Nothing ever fails. Children sort coins and pick between saving and spending.

Ghana (cedi)
Sort pictures of cedi coins into jars.
Nigeria (naira)
Sort pictures of naira coins into jars.
United Kingdom (pound)
Sort pictures of pound coins into jars.

Ages 7 to 9

Build & Grow

Short sentences. Children run a small stand, set a price and save towards a goal. Try again is always free.

Ghana (cedi)
Price a cup of sobolo at the school fair.
Nigeria (naira)
Price a bottle of zobo at the school fair.
United Kingdom (pound)
Price a cup of lemonade at the school fair.

Ages 10 to 12

Founder Mode

Full sentences and simple real words such as profit, loan and share of a business. Children compare more than one good plan.

Ghana (cedi)
Choose how to fund a phone-charging kiosk.
Nigeria (naira)
Choose how to fund a snack-packing business.
United Kingdom (pound)
Choose how to fund a bike-cleaning round.

Ages 13 to 15

Side Hustle

Goals, trade-offs and honest numbers. Temptations to spend show how they change a savings plan.

Ghana (cedi)
Save for a school trip while a sale tempts you.
Nigeria (naira)
Save for a laptop while a sale tempts you.
United Kingdom (pound)
Save for a festival ticket while a sale tempts you.

All amounts in the game are pretend coins. The examples above only show how the pictures and prices change with where you live.
