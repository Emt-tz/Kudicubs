How KudiCubs works

A parent sets it up once. Then children play short missions and earn pretend coins and badges.

  1. A parent sets up the family

    You choose your child's nickname, age band and where you live. You also set a parent PIN. Nothing more is needed.

  2. Children play missions

    Each mission takes a few minutes. Kudi the Cub guides your child with a spoken voice, so younger children do not need to read.

  3. They earn coins and badges

    Coins and badges are pretend. They cannot be turned into real money, and there is nothing to buy.

  4. You follow along

    The Parent Dashboard shows what your child played and which skills they are building.

Five skills, learned through play

Every mission builds one or more of these.

Know

Recognise coins and notes, and what things cost.

Understand

See where money comes from and where it goes.

Decide

Weigh choices, such as save it or spend it.

Invest

Learn that small amounts can grow over time.

Share

Give, help others and use money kindly.
