How we keep children safe

Plain words, no small print. Children play. Parents stay in charge.

A parent gate

Settings, links that leave the app and anything about your account sit behind a gate that a young child cannot pass by chance.

A parent PIN

You choose a PIN when you set up. Only people who know it can change settings or see account details.

No real money

Coins in KudiCubs are pretend. They cannot be bought, sold or turned into real money. Children cannot make purchases.

No open chat

Children cannot type messages to anyone or share anything with other players. There is no stranger to talk to.

Very little data

We keep a nickname, an age band and game progress. We do not ask for a child's real name, photo, contacts or location.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads and no tracking tools in the game or on this website. This website sets no cookies.
