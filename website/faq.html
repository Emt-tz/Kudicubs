What is KudiCubs?

A play-and-learn app that teaches children aged 4 to 15 about money through short missions with Kudi the Cub, a friendly lion.

Which ages is it for?

Ages 4 to 15, in four bands: 4 to 6, 7 to 9, 10 to 12 and 13 to 15. Each band has its own level of reading and thinking.

Is real money involved?

No. Coins are pretend and cannot be bought, sold or turned into real money. Children cannot make purchases.

Can my child chat with other people?

No. There is no chat and nothing children write is shared with anyone.

What do you keep about my child?

A nickname, an age band and game progress. No real name, photo, contacts or location.

Are there ads?

No ads, in the app or on this website. We also use no tracking tools.

Which countries does it suit?

Examples use the Ghana cedi, the Nigeria naira and the UK pound. More places will follow.

How do I see how my child is doing?

Sign in to the Parent Dashboard with a one-time code sent to your email. It shows what was played and which skills are growing.

How do I get the app?

Use the Get the app page. The store listings are coming soon, because this is a prototype.

How do I delete my family's information?

The Parent Dashboard has a data page for export and deletion. You can also contact us.
