# 10–12 first mission: Secure start-up funding for a clothing brand

Source: `docs/source/BIZ KIDS Content.pptx`, slides 13 and 15 (python-pptx, T004).

**Concepts**: equity vs debt, raising capital (group: also financial planning, market and competitor analysis).

**Steps (deck)**: the player pitches a business idea to investors, banks or partners and chooses debt financing (a bank loan) or equity financing (investment funding). Balloon pop-ups explain the concepts as they play.

**Decision moment (deck)**: choose presentation slides, budget plans, revenue forecasts and marketing strategies. After the money arrives, a mini game (crisis manager) solves unexpected problems such as product shortage or negative reviews.

**Outcome / learning (deck)**: equity gives investors part ownership; debt must be repaid with interest; each option has risks and benefits.

**Regional examples**: none in the deck. The prototype takes the product name from the region's `marketExamples`.

## How the prototype builds it (routes `m10-*`)

| Route | What happens |
|---|---|
| `m10-intro` | Start a clothing brand; you need 100 coins to begin |
| `m10-resource` | Raise the money: bank loan (repay 110), investor (they take 20% of the profit), own savings (only 50 coins) |
| `m10-market` | Set a price for one month (weeks 1 to 4 as a light time cue); sales depend on price and how many you could make |
| `m10-result` | Sales, production cost, cost of the money, net result. A weak month: calm explanation, **Try again** first |
| `m10-summary` | Equity vs debt in one line each |

Not built: the pitch slides and the crisis-manager mini game.

**Deviation from the task's placeholder default** ("The Lemonade Stand"): the deck was readable, so its real first 10–12 mission is used. The route ids `m10-resource` and `m10-market` fit it (resource management, market simulation).
