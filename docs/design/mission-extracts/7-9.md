# 7–9 first mission: Budget for your birthday party

Source: `docs/source/BIZ KIDS Content.pptx`, slides 8 and 9 (read with python-pptx, T004). Extracted 2026-11-20.

**Concept**: budgeting (group concepts: budgeting, profit and loss, marketing choices, competition).

**Steps (deck)**: the player receives an amount of money and budgets for a birthday party (cake, party essentials), staying under a coin limit by dragging items into a cart.

**Decision moment (deck)**: choose between expensive and affordable items. Deck examples: drinks at 10 or at 50, cake at 20 (the deck writes these in cedis).

**Outcome / learning (deck)**: the player learns what a budget is, and that spending without a budget leads to bad decisions.

**Regional examples**: the deck names Ghana only (cedis). The prototype uses coins (the region's coin picture) and takes drink, snack and treat names from `KC.regions[...].marketExamples`; no currency name is hard-coded.

## How the prototype builds it (routes `m7-*`)

| Route | What happens |
|---|---|
| `m7-intro` | Kudi: let us plan a party; budget 60 coins |
| `m7-decision` | Pick a party plan (3 cards): fancy drinks 70 coins; smart plan 45; simple plan 30 |
| `m7-result` | Cost against budget, coins left. Over budget: calm explanation, **Try again** first |
| `m7-summary` | What a budget is and what the child chose |

Not built (one mission per band in this round): the drag-to-cart step. The deck's Mission 2 "The Lemonade challenge" (profit and loss) is the natural next 7–9 mission.
