# 13–15 first mission: Side hustle simulator

Source: `docs/source/BIZ KIDS Content.pptx`, slides 19, 20 and 22 (python-pptx, T004).

**Concepts**: entrepreneurship and budgeting (group: budgeting and planning, saving and delayed gratification).

**Steps (deck)**: choose a side hustle (sneaker reselling, smoothie stand, fashion brand, graphic design, mobile snack cart). Manage startup costs, supplies, marketing, pricing, customer reviews and social media popularity.

**Fun feature (deck)**: "Temptation Alerts" (pop-ups to spend now or save for a bigger reward). Not built in this mission.

**Regional examples**: none in the deck. The prototype's stand is the region's drink example.

## How the prototype builds it (routes `m13-*`)

| Route | What happens |
|---|---|
| `m13-intro` | Mentor tone: a smoothie-style drink stand; budget 100 coins; cost, price, profit |
| `m13-budget` | Choose a batch (10, 20 or 25 cups at 4 coins each) against the budget |
| `m13-market` | Choose a price (6, 9 or 12 coins); demand falls as price rises |
| `m13-result` | Revenue, costs, profit or loss, margin, budget left; a loss is explained, **Try again** first |
| `m13-summary` | Cost, price, profit and margin in one line each |

Only one hustle is built (the deck lists five). All numbers are game coins, not money.
