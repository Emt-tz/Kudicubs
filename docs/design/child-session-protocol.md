# Optional Child Session: Protocol

The goal is to see whether 4–6 year olds can play **The Lost Coins** in the prototype **without
being read to**: spec 002 US5 and SC-009. This session is **optional** for client sign-off.

> **⚠ Round 1 (spec 003): wait for the recordings.** Sessions with children **must not run until
> the human voice recordings exist** for The Lost Coins (`en-GH`; check the reviewer menu's
> "Recordings" count). Pre-readers can't use the "Recording pending" captions, so a session
> without audio would test the captions, not the game (plan 003, flag N1).

## 1. Consent first (Constitution V)

- [ ] **Each parent or guardian is present and agrees before anything starts.** Read aloud:
  > "We're testing a game idea for children about money. Your child will play for about
  > 5 minutes on this phone. It's a test of the *game*, not of your child: there are no wrong
  > answers. We won't record their face or voice, and we'll only note their first name or
  > initials and age. You can stop at any time."
- [ ] Ask separately, and only if needed: "May we take notes on paper?" (yes or no).
- [ ] **No photos, video or audio recording** unless the parent separately agrees in writing to
  that specific recording, for a stated purpose.
- [ ] No personal details beyond a **child code** (C1, C2…), **age** and **first name or
  initials**. No surnames, schools or contact details.
- [ ] The child can stop at any moment, and that is respected immediately.

## 2. Setup

- A phone with the **sound on** (volume about 70%), the prototype opened from the link, in
  portrait.
- Before each child: **hold the logo for 2 s → Restart prototype**, then **Jump to → Home**.
  The child starts on **Home**; the parent gate is skipped, because it is for grown-ups.
- A quiet corner, with the parent sitting beside the child.

## 3. Facilitator rules

- **Don't read text aloud, don't point, and don't tap for the child.** Kudi's voice and the
  pictures must do the work.
- If the child asks for help, say only: "What do you think Kudi wants you to do?"
- If the child is stuck for **more than 30 seconds**, you may say: "Listen to Kudi", and tap
  the speaker button once. Note it as an assist.
- Praise effort ("You're doing great!"), never correctness.

## 4. What to note

Use [child-session-results.md](./child-session-results.md):

| Per screen | Note |
|---|---|
| Hesitation | Did the child pause for **more than 10 s** without acting? (Y/N) |
| First tap | Where did they tap first? Was it the main action? |
| Gentle feedback | Did they see a bounce or hint? How did they react (smiled, tried again, upset)? |
| Assist | Did the facilitator need to help? |
| Reached Reward | Y/N |

## 5. Stop rules

Stop and thank the child straight away if they are **upset, bored or want to stop**. Don't push
to finish. A stopped session is recorded as "did not reach Reward: stopped by child". That is
useful information, not a failure.

## 6. Thank you

Give a printed KudiCubs sticker or a colouring sheet. **Never money or gift cards** (Constitution
V: no real money).
