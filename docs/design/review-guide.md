*Round 1 guide (still accurate):*

Thank you for your feedback on the first preview. This **round-1 prototype (v0.2.0)** does the
three things you asked for:

1. **Your official brand**: the KudiCubs logo, Kudi the Cub's five official poses, and the Sunshine
   Yellow, Mane Brown and orange colours.
2. **No more robot voice**: Kudi will speak with a **real, recorded human voice**. The recordings
   haven't been made yet. Until they are, The Lost Coins uses the temporary practice voice
   described above.
3. **Much more to do**: children now drag, tap and answer on every screen, and Kudi reacts every
   time.

It takes about **5–7 minutes**. To see exactly what changed on each screen, open
[round1-before-after.md](./round1-before-after.md).

## 1. Open it

- **On your phone (best)**: open the link, in portrait, with the **sound on**.

  **Link**: **https://emt-tz.github.io/Kudicubs/** (the same link every time; it updates by itself)

- To keep it like an app, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** on iPhone, or **⋮ → Add to Home
  screen** on Android. After the first visit it works without internet.
- **On a laptop**: unzip `prototype-v0.3.0.zip` and double-click `index.html`.

## 2. Try these new things

| Where | What to try |
|---|---|
| **Every screen** | The orange **↻ button** (top right) replays what Kudi said. |
| **Welcome** | Tap **Kudi** to hear hello, or tap the **logo** for "Play. Earn. Roar!". |
| **Age and friends** | Tap each card. Each one speaks. |
| **The Lost Coins intro** | Kudi asks **"Can you help me find them?"**. Tap 👍 to start. |
| **Find** | Each coin you tap **says its name** ("Fifty pesewas!"). |
| **Sort** | **Drag the coins with your finger** into the jars. Watch each jar's **count go up** with every coin. Try a **wrong jar**: Kudi gently encourages (fist pose) and the coin floats back. No failure. |
| **Count** | Each coin says its number. Then Kudi asks **"Which jar has the most coins?"**. Tap a jar. |
| **Save or spend** | Tap each card to hear it. The treat changes by country. |
| **Result** | Tap the jar or the sticker to finish the moment. |
| **Summary** | Kudi asks **"What did you do with your coins?"**. Tap your answer. |
| **Reward** | Tap the rewards to celebrate again. Tap badges to hear them. |
| **Wallet and Progress** | Tap anything to hear it. |

**Switch country**: hold the **KudiCubs logo at the top for 2 seconds** to open the grown-up menu.
There you can switch Ghana, Nigeria or the UK, choose the voice language, jump to any screen, or
restart.

## 4. Decisions we need from you

Short answers are perfect. A WhatsApp voice note or text is fine.

1. **Yellow logo**: the yellow version has a white tagline, which is hard to read. Is there a
   version with a brown tagline?
2. **Peace-sign pose**: none of the five poses shows a peace sign. Can we use the **wink pose with
   the sparkling coin** for celebrating, or will you send a peace-sign pose?
3. **Success green**: the guide asks for a separate green for success messages. We suggest
   **#16793A**. OK?
4. **Warm Cream**: the guide's code for Warm Cream is a typo (#F68C1F is the orange). We used
   **#FEF5E0** from the swatch. Correct?
5. **Charcoal**: #000000 or #231F20?
6. **Pressed buttons**: we use slightly darker yellow and orange when buttons are pressed, and a
   lighter brown for small text. OK?
7. **Name in text**: should the app say "KudiCubs", "Kudicubs" or "Kudicub's"?
8. **Master files**: please send the original logo (vector) and the five mascot images
   (transparent PNG).
9. **Fonts**: we suggest Baloo 2 and Nunito, which are free and licensed for apps, for buttons and
   text. OK?
10. **Draft art colours**: our draft coins and scenes use a few extra colours (silver, bronze, flag
    colours, greens) until the final illustrations. OK?
11. **App icon**: for the phone's home-screen icon we used just **the cub and K coin** from your
    yellow logo, because the full logo is too small to read at that size. Nothing is redrawn. OK?
12. **First voice**: shall we record **Ghanaian English** first?

Thank you! 🦁
