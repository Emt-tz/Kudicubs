# KudiCubs Prototype: Review Guide

Hello Jefferson 👋

This is a **clickable preview** of KudiCubs. It looks and feels like the real app will, but it's
not the app yet. It lets us agree on the look, the feel and the flow **before** we build. It
takes about **5 minutes**.

## 1. Open it

- **On your phone (best)**: open this link. Use it in portrait, with the **sound on**.

  **Link**: *(added here once the prototype is published)*

- To keep it like an app, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** on iPhone, or **⋮ → Add to Home
  screen** on Android. After the first visit it works **without internet** too.
- **On a laptop**: unzip `prototype-v0.1.0.zip` and double-click `index.html`. It shows as a
  phone in the middle of the screen.

## 2. Tap through it like a 4–6 year old

Follow Kudi the Cub. Each screen has **one big button** or one set of pictures to tap:

1. **Welcome**: tap *Let's Go*.
2. **Age**: tap *4–6*. The other ages say "Coming soon", because those worlds come in later
   stages.
3. **Pick a friend** and a name.
4. **For grown-ups**: hold the gold circle for 3 seconds, answer the sum, choose any 4-digit
   PIN, choose your country, and type any email. **Nothing you type is saved or sent.**
5. **Home**: tap *Continue*.
6. **The Lost Coins**: find the 8 coins in the market, put each coin in its matching jar, then
   count them. Try putting a coin in the **wrong jar** to see how gently Kudi helps.
7. **Save it or spend it?** Both are good choices. Try both if you like.
8. See the **result**, the **summary** and your **rewards** (with a Level Up!).
9. At the bottom, tap **Wallet** and **Progress**.

## 3. Switch countries

**Hold the "KudiCubs" logo at the top for 2 seconds.** A grown-up menu opens. There you can:

- Switch between **Ghana**, **Nigeria** and the **United Kingdom**. The coins change.
- **Jump** to any screen.
- **Restart** the preview.

## 4. What's placeholder (please don't judge these yet)

- **The drawings** of Kudi, the friends, the coins and the market are **draft art**. They show
  the *direction* (a lion cub, a warm market, local coins), not the final illustrations.
- **The voice** is your phone's built-in robot voice. Real voices from Ghana, Nigeria and the UK
  will be recorded later. Please judge **when** Kudi speaks, not how the voice sounds.
- The numbers (coins, levels, badges) are made up.

## 5. What we'd love your thoughts on

1. **Look and feel**: does it feel warm, fun and African-market? Anything too busy or too plain?
2. **Kudi the Cub**: do you like him as the guide? Anything to change about his look or
   personality?
3. **Colours**: gold, sand, terracotta, indigo and green. Do they feel right for KudiCubs?
4. **The flow**: is it always clear what to tap next? Was any screen confusing?
5. **The coins**: do the Ghana, Nigeria and UK coins feel local enough?
6. **Anything missing** that you expected to see?

**How to reply**: a WhatsApp **voice note or text** is perfect. Screenshots of anything you want
to point at help a lot.

Thank you! 🦁
